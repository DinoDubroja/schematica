## What it demonstrates

A bias-divider column sits left of an NPN transistor. A top horizontal rail labeled $V_{\mathrm{CC}}$ feeds the upper resistor and transistor collector. The input couples horizontally through $C_1$ to the divider/base region. The emitter node branches right through $C_2$ and downward through $R_{\mathrm{E}}$ to ground. The divider's lower resistor has its own local ground.

Zigzag resistors, straight capacitor plates, a circle-free transistor symbol, filled junction dots, and subscripted component identifiers form a compact conventional schematic. The base connection and input-capacitor connection use distinct, closely spaced dots on the same divider wire. Labels sit beside resistors/transistor and below the horizontal capacitors, with clearance from the wiring. Input and output leads end as plain lines in this reference.
