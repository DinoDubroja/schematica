The left reference uses D1, LM385-1.2, and R1, 100k; the R2, 100k, potentiometer's wiper runs horizontally to the op-amp's lower, positive input. A vertical $V_{\mathrm{in}}$ span annotation between rail and wiper is separate from the wires. The op-amp has visible supply connections to the rail and ground.
