## What it demonstrates

The figure reads from left to right: transformer, diamond-shaped four-diode bridge, shunt polarized capacitor, rectangular regulator block, and exposed output connections. The bridge's diagonal diode symbols follow the diamond geometry while the surrounding wires use horizontal and vertical runs. Filled dots mark the four bridge vertices and the capacitor/input branch.

The word regulator sits above the block. Its in, out, and ground labels sit inside, adjacent to the corresponding terminals. The regulator ground connection drops below the block. The capacitor has a straight upper plate, curved lower plate, and a plus sign by the upper side. Ground symbols repeat near the bridge, capacitor, regulator, and separate output-return terminal. Hollow circles mark both the output and its return connection on the right.

## Inferred lessons to review

- A figure can combine explicit components with a functional block when internal block detail is unnecessary to its explanation.
- Placing terminal labels inside the block and the block name outside distinguishes the two kinds of text.
- Repeated local grounds and a largely horizontal arrangement keep the illustration compact and readable.
- Diagonal device orientation can make a bridge topology clear without forcing every component onto horizontal or vertical axes.

These observations remain provisional. No new ground-symbol, block-label, or routing preference is adopted by this record.
