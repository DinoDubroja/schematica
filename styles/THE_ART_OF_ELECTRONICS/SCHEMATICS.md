# The Art of Electronics — schematics

Read [the shared style guide](STYLE.md) and applicable [general preferences](../../preferences/GENERAL.md) first. This guide collects existing circuit-specific observations; the reorganization does not adopt new drawing rules or numeric defaults.

## Scope and status

Use for practical circuits, conceptual connection diagrams, circuit comparisons, and the circuit portion of mixed illustrations. The reference records and five visual recreations retain the asset/review status listed in the [collection inventory](STYLE.md#example-guide). Patterns below remain provisional; technical connectivity, names, values, and roles come from the requested circuit.

## Lines and symbols — provisional

Use restrained monochrome line drawings, clear symbols, and consistent proportions. Keep weights consistent within their roles; waveform traces and connection dots may be stronger than fine annotation lines. The selected circuit examples use zigzag resistors, simple capacitor plates, and uncluttered ground bars. Follow the relevant reference for geometry.

Distinguish filled junction dots, exposed terminals, annotation arrows, and signal traces. Do not transfer a symbol or annotation convention without its meaning.

For composition, labels, typography, and combined panels, use the [shared observations](STYLE.md#shared-visual-character-provisional-observations). Exact fonts, spacing, symbol proportions, and line weights remain unsettled.
