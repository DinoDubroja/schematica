## What it demonstrates

Two inward-facing transistor symbols sit below a horizontal $+V_{\mathrm{CC}}$ rail. The left collector connects directly to that rail; the right collector connects through $R_{\mathrm{C}}$ and has an unlabeled output lead. A two-line dc input (noninverting) label sits beside the left base. The right base has a local ground connection.

Two horizontal emitter resistors join at a central filled dot above a circular current-source symbol. The downward arrow is inside that circle, and the tail lead ends with the label $-V_{\mathrm{EE}}$. The emitter-resistor label is symbolic; no numeric values are shown. Wide spacing between the transistor columns gives the interior connections room while placing the grounded reference input outside the pair.
